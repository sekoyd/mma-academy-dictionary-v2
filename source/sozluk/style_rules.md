# MMA Academy Dictionary: style pass rules (2026-09-23)

Register: MMA Academy speaking to marketers in Türkiye and MEA. Institutional, clear, neutral reference voice.
Reader's single action: understand the term fast and remember it.

## Hard constraints (fact-checked content)
- Do not add, remove, or change any fact: names, companies, titles, dates, numbers, statistics, quotes, attributions, sources, origins.
- Do not add examples or claims. Only rewrite wording.
- Leave `term`, `notion_title`, `folder`, `related_terms`, `tr_equivalent`, `sources`, `notes`, `category`, `blog_language` untouched.
- No em-dashes (—). No curly quotes; use straight quotes.
- Dictionary voice: no first person, no opinions, no jokes. (The humanizer's "add personality" advice does not apply here.)

## Pass 1: humanizer (fields: short_definition, long_explanation, usage_context, tr_definition)
Remove these AI-writing patterns:
- Inflated significance: "plays a crucial/pivotal/key role", "marks a shift", "evolving landscape", "testament to", "underscores".
- AI vocabulary: additionally, crucial, delve, enhance, fostering, garner, highlight (verb), interplay, intricate, key (adj), landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, tapestry, underscore, valuable, vibrant, leverage, seamless, robust, unlock, harness, game-changer.
- Copula avoidance: "serves as / stands as / represents" -> "is".
- Negative parallelism: "not just X, it's Y", "not about X but Y" (keep only if the contrast is the definition itself).
- Forced rule of three; synonym cycling; false "from X to Y" ranges.
- Trailing -ing clauses that add fake depth ("..., highlighting the importance of ...").
- Vague attribution ("experts say", "industry reports") unless a named source is already in the text.
- Filler ("it is important to note", "in order to") and excess hedging.
- Generic upbeat closing sentences.
- Formula tics: every long_explanation ending with "For marketers, ... matters because ...". Vary how the marketer relevance is stated; keep the content.
Keep sentences of varied length. Plain words.

## Pass 2: neuro-linguist (fields: short_definition, tr_definition only)
Critique-and-strengthen with these lenses:
1. Processing fluency: short, familiar words; concrete image over abstraction; cut jargon the term itself does not need.
2. Syntax: lead with what the thing IS (genus + differentia). Put the core noun early. One sentence, ideally under 25 words (TR: natural Turkish, not a word-for-word translation).
3. Concreteness: prefer a concrete noun or action over a nominalization.
4. Load cleaning: remove filler, weak verbs, nominalizations ("the practice of the utilization of" -> "using"). Turkish: avoid stacked "-mektedir/-maktadır" and "olarak ifade edilmektedir"; prefer "-dır" or active verb endings; avoid calques like "önemli bir rol oynar".
5. Framing: neutral and accurate. No hype, no fear, no false urgency.
6. Figures: none in definitions unless the term is itself a wordplay (then explain the blend plainly).
Turkish must use correct characters (ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü) and read as native marketing Turkish.
