# Term of the Week: 2026-W40

**Pick:** Frankenstack (Technology Trends)
**Definition:** A marketing or advertising technology stack assembled from many separately bought tools that were bolted together over time and do not work as one system.
**Hook:** MMA's Decoding AI for Marketing podcast (Greg Stuart, Rex Briggs), episode "Can AI Finally Fix Advertising's Frankenstack?", 8 Sep 2026, guest Mike Lane (CEO, Fluency).

## Why it won
- Primary source: Greg Stuart's own podcast, episode 20 days old (inside the 3-week window).
- Clear, verified origin: chiefmartec.com (Scott Brinker), 31 Mar 2015; CIO.com interview, 18 May 2015.
- Directly relevant to brand and agency teams in Türkiye/MEA adding AI tools to mixed global/local stacks.
